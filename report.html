Does near-duplicate antigen homology move the CoFold Arena Ab–Ag leaderboard?

422 antibody–antigen targets, 18 leaderboard models, MSA-mode DockQ. Targets are grouped by antigen sequence homology (MMseqs2), each group gets equal weight, and the results are compared with the published, unweighted leaderboard. Data snapshot: bundle index-D1t1x7qL.js, fetched 2026-11-02.

Short answer: duplication doesn't change who's on top, but it does make the top more decisive. Mol-1 stays #1 under every weighting, and the top four (Mol-1, OpenDDE, OpenDDE-AbAg, Protenix-v2) stay the top four. Under antigen-50% weighting Mol-1's lead over OpenDDE grows from 0.004 to 0.013 DockQ. Its bootstrap probability of being #1 rises from 0.51 to 0.70 (0.82 at 30%), because the largest homology groups (influenza HA, HLA class I) are ones where Mol-1 does relatively poorly. Even so, the Mol-1 vs OpenDDE gap is not resolved: its 95% CI is -0.020 to +0.047 at 50% and -0.011 to +0.054 at 30%.

Most of the effect sits mid-table, where models are 0.01 DockQ apart. IntelliFold-2-Flash climbs 3–4 places and Boltz-2 drops three; the other moves are one or two places among models whose rank intervals overlap. Weighting raises nearly every model's score (mean +0.010 at 30%), because the over-represented antigen families are harder than average. Scores move by at most ~0.02 DockQ. The honest error bars widen by about 1.11× (cluster bootstrap with weighting vs the naive per-target bootstrap).

No single group shifts a top-6 model by more than ~0.006 DockQ, or any model by more than ~0.007 (0.010 for the merged 26-target HA group in the shorter-chain-coverage grouping). Dropping one group never changes who is #1. Dropping the neuraminidase group (6 Fabs, OpenDDE 0.42 vs OpenDDE-AbAg 0.21) swaps #2 and #3. The SARS-CoV-2 XBB.1.5 RBD/S1 group (Mol-1 0.06 vs OpenDDE-AbAg 0.73) and the HA and HLA groups move the top models in opposite directions. Null: 1,000 random reassignments of targets to groups with the same size distribution. It asks whether the reshuffle comes from homology itself or would happen under any reweighting this uneven. At 30% the observed τ is lower than almost every null draw. At 50% and 90% it is within the range of noise. No single model's relative gain under weighting is individually significant against the null, Mol-1's included. "CI width" is the mean bootstrap SD of the weighted score over the per-target bootstrap SD of the raw score. The raw score's own cluster-bootstrap SD is only 1.02× the naive one at 50%, so the published CIs are only slightly overconfident.

Method and assumptions

  • Data. The Cd (targets), He (scores) and Pd (models) literals were brace-matched out of the bundle and evaluated in an empty vm context. Leaderboard = msa scores, esmfold2_fast excluded (it has no msa scores). Every published check reproduces exactly: Mol-1 0.359 / 48.8% acceptable, OpenDDE 0.355, Protenix-v2 0.327, Boltz-2 0.175, AF-Multimer 0.106.
  • Sequences. Taken from the RCSB GraphQL API as SEQRES canonical sequences. All 420 PDB entries resolved, including 12PG, 46GH, 10ND and 13FL, which are ordinary 4-character IDs (new digit-leading issue) and not extended IDs. The chain IDs are auth IDs: read as label IDs, 309 of 1,223 lookups don't exist and 257 hit a different entity. Two PDB entries contribute two antibody targets each. No target was dropped.
  • Antigen clustering. mmseqs easy-cluster --min-seq-id {0.3,0.5,0.9} -c 0.8 --cov-mode 0 -s 7.5 on the unique antigen chains of each target (462 sequences), then union-find over targets. Only the chains the Arena lists as antigen are used. For pMHC targets that means the MHC heavy chain and peptide (sometimes β2m), so different peptides on HLA-A2 group together through the HLA chain.
  • Coverage caveat. Bidirectional 80% coverage keeps HA1-only constructs and full-length HA0 apart, and likewise RBD vs ectodomain. The spec'd thresholds therefore under-merge sub-construct duplicates. The extra “shorter-chain coverage” grouping links two targets when identity ≥50% and the shorter chain is ≥80% covered (all-vs-all easy-search). That grouping merges 26 HA targets (HA1-only and full-length) into one group and gives results close to antigen-30%.
  • Antibody clustering. V domains were trimmed with ANARCI (IMGT) before clustering at 90% identity, so that Fab CH1 domains don't merge unrelated Fabs. The two shark VNARs (not modelled by ANARCI) fall back to their first 125 residues. Joint group = identical (antigen-50% group, VH-90% cluster) pair. The VH clusters with mixed antigens (e.g. a 5-member cluster of synthetic-library Fabs) are why the joint grouping also requires a shared antigen group.
  • Scoring. Weight = 1/group size. The weighted mean was asserted equal to the mean of group means for every grouping, metric and subset. The bootstrap uses B=2,000 and seed 20261102; every model is scored on the same resample, and rank intervals are 2.5–97.5 percentiles of the per-resample rank.
  • Not done. No epitope-level clustering: two antibodies against different epitopes of the same HA still share a group. No training-set overlap analysis (which models saw homologues before their cutoffs). This analysis measures within-benchmark redundancy only.
